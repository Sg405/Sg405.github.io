Grading Process

Grade with intent, not with presets.

Every job starts from scratch. Presets create visual shortcuts, and shortcuts show. My process is methodical — primary correction first, then scene matching, then the creative grade that gives the piece its character.

  • LOG footage primary correction (lift/gamma/gain)
  • Skin tone isolation & protection via qualifier
  • Cohesive scene-to-scene matching
  • Product highlight refinement
  • LUT development for brand consistency

Tools I work in.

Primarily DaVinci Resolve for color work — the node-based workflow lets me build grades that are logical, reversible, and easy to hand off. Premiere Pro Lumetri for edits that need to stay in the same timeline.

  • DaVinci Resolve — color page (primary tool)
  • Premiere Pro — Lumetri Color, editing
  • After Effects — title animation, motion graphics
  • LOG formats: S-Log, C-Log, V-Log
Typography

Type is design.
Treat it like one.

On-screen text in tech reviews is often the cheapest-looking element of otherwise expensive productions. Typeface selection, weight, tracking, and animation all need to serve the brand's register — not a template's defaults.

  • Typeface selection aligned to product tier
  • Motion that reveals, not distracts
  • Negative space as a design element
  • Consistent hierarchy across all frames
