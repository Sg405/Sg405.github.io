Biography

I'm Saloni - a Delhi-based editor and colorist with a background in English literature and a deep fixation on the craft of visual storytelling. My path into post-production came through a decade of obsessively reading, writing, and editing long-form text, which gave me something most colorists don't have: a structural understanding of how meaning is built layer by layer.

That editorial sensibility is what I bring to every finishing job. Color grading isn't just correcting exposure - it's setting the emotional register of a piece. Typography isn't just adding text — it's extending the brand into every frame.

I work primarily with tech review and gadget content, where the standard is high and the margin for error is zero. Audiences in this space are visually literate — they notice when something looks off, even if they can't name it.

Available for per-project work and monthly retainer arrangements. I turn around fast, I communicate clearly, and I don't miss frames.
